# Fruit Box

Fruit Box is a number puzzle in which players select rectangles whose remaining fruit values total ten. These terms distinguish the proposed solo and multiplayer experiences.

## Language

**Player**:
A participant in solo attempts or multiplayer matches, whether playing as a guest or using an account.

**Guest player**:
A player participating without signing into an account.

**Nickname**:
A player's displayed name; sharing a nickname does not make two players the same person.

**Board**:
A rectangular arrangement of numbered fruit and the empty spaces left after fruit is removed.

**Selection**:
A rectangle a player proposes to clear from a board.

**Clear**:
The removal of the remaining fruit inside a valid selection.

**Score**:
The number of fruit credited to a player or team through clears, rather than the sum of their printed values.

**Shared-board competition**:
A live match in which players remove fruit from one common board and earn separate scores. Fruit cleared by one player is unavailable to everyone else.
_Avoid_: Race, same-board race

**Separate-board race**:
A live match in which each player plays an independent copy of the same initial board and compares scores.
_Avoid_: Shared-board competition

**Friend challenge**:
An asynchronous comparison of attempts on the same initial board under the same rules.

**Shared-board cooperation**:
A live match in which players clear fruit from one common board toward a combined score.

**Attempt**:
One player's playthrough of a board under a particular set of rules.

**Room**:
A private gathering of players who can start matches together.

**Match**:
A competition or cooperative session played under one agreed set of rules.
